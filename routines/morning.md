# Morning check

Goal: a short readiness note and a confirmed (or adjusted) session for today.

1. `bin/icu days 7`. Find today's date on the ribbon.
2. `bin/icu wellness <today−28d> <today>`. Compare today's HRV, resting HR and sleep
   against the athlete's own 7-day and 28-day baseline (reference: "Readiness gates").
   - Tracker but no row for today yet → say it hasn't synced and lean on the trend.
   - No tracker at all → don't mention HRV or sleep. Use form (ctl − atl),
     yesterday's session, and ask how they slept and how the legs feel.
   - Never present yesterday's numbers as today's.
3. `bin/icu activities <yesterday> <today>`: what did they actually do?
4. `bin/icu events <today> <today+2d>`: what's planned today and next?
5. Decide: keep, soften, swap or move today's session (readiness rules from the
   reference). Resting HR ≥5 bpm above baseline, or HRV clearly suppressed for
   two or more days, means no intensity today. Make the smallest change that works.
6. If you change the calendar, show the change and get an OK (AGENTS.md → Calendar etiquette).
7. Reply in 1–3 sentences: the readiness read and today's session with its
   key numbers. Nothing more unless today is a key day.
