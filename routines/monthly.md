# Monthly review

Audit the last 4–5 weeks honestly.

1. `bin/icu activities`, `bin/icu wellness` and `bin/icu events` for the last 35 days;
   power or pace curve: `bin/icu get athlete/0/power-curves curves=84d type=Ride`
   (or `pace-curves … type=Run`).
2. Prescribed vs completed, CTL/eFTP trend, readiness response to load, what the
   athlete pushed back on, session feedback in LEARNINGS.md.
3. Refresh USER.md → Strengths & weaknesses (dated, with numbers, both the data side
   and the athlete side). Check test staleness: is the anchor older than 4–6 weeks?
4. Update LEARNINGS.md (dosing notes, validated or refuted) and prune stale lines.
5. Short honest report to the athlete: what worked, what didn't, what changes.
