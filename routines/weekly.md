# Weekly review (Sunday evening is a good time)

1. `bin/icu days 7`. Pull last week: `bin/icu activities`, `bin/icu wellness`,
   `bin/icu events` for Mon–Sun.
2. Count it: sessions, hours and load per sport (sum the activities yourself
   and show the totals). Prescribed vs completed.
3. Check due hypotheses in LEARNINGS.md: move them to Validated or Refuted with
   the evidence.
4. Look at the phase and weeks to the A-race in PLAN.md. Is next week's
   structure still right? Deload due?
5. Message, about 5 sentences: how the week went, one thing that stood out,
   what next week is for. Then offer to run `routines/plan.md` if next week
   isn't booked yet.
6. Optionally pin a short week summary as a NOTE on Sunday.
