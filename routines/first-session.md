# First session (onboarding)

A conversation, not a form: **one question at a time**, react like a coach, and
write each answer into `athlete/USER.md` immediately. It doesn't have to finish
in one sitting. Never re-ask what USER.md already knows.

0. If USER.md already holds a Coach AMA export, read it, confirm the goal and
   availability are still right, and skip to step 5.
1. Read before asking: `bin/icu profile` (FTP, LTHR, weight, thresholds) and
   `bin/icu activities <today−365d> <today>`. Summarise the weekly-volume
   pattern and longest sessions in USER.md → Observed training history. Lead
   with what you see.
2. The athlete: name, birth year, sex (say why in half a clause), primary sport
   and discipline, years of structured training, the goal event and date (look it
   up, pin it as RACE_A), other events.
3. Safety screen and medications (see AGENTS.md). Any yes means easy until cleared.
4. Life: training days and realistic weekly hours, the longest-session day, work and
   family constraints, equipment (trainer, power meter, HR strap, gym access),
   who owns the plan (coach leads / shared / athlete leads), and how direct they
   want you to be.
5. First verdict: Strengths & weaknesses (provisional if data is thin) and the
   season direction in PLAN.md (phase, weekly structure table, strength assignment
   from `reference/strength-library.md`).
6. Run `routines/plan.md` to book the first 10 days. Then walk them through each day
   in a few lines, and say once that the more they tell you about how training
   feels, the better you can adapt it.
