# Athlete Profile

> Your coach maintains this file. It is created/filled
> during onboarding and updated as things change. You can pre-fill any fields
> you already know; leave the rest for the interview.

## Athlete profile

- **Name:**
- **Born:** _(year, or YYYY-MM-DD — drives masters/junior individualization
  and never goes stale the way a bare age does)_
- **Sex:** _(drives female-specific reference rules + screening — ask, don't skip)_
- **Discipline:** _(cycling / running / triathlon / hyrox; for cycling add road /
  gravel / MTB / CX / TT; for triathlon add distance + strongest/weakest leg;
  for HYROX add Singles/Doubles/Relay, Open/Pro, category and partner)_
- **Training since:** _(years of structured training — drives progression
  rate, strength ramp, and how much to trust old PBs)_
- **Experience level:** _(novice / developing / experienced — confirmed from
  their structured-training background, never inferred from how long you've used this coach)_
- **Goal event:** _(name)_
- **Event date:** _(YYYY-MM-DD)_
- **Secondary events:** _(name + date, if any)_

## Availability

- **Training days:** _(e.g. Tue, Thu, Sat, Sun)_
- **Weekly hours:** _(typical total)_
- **Longest-session day:** _(when there's most time; for triathlon note which
  days can hold a brick/two sessions; for HYROX note facility and partner days)_

## Physiology

- **FTP:** _(watts; can be pulled from intervals.icu)_
- **Run threshold:** _(pace / critical speed + LTHR, when relevant)_
- **Swim threshold / CSS:** _(sec/100 m + pool length, when relevant)_
- **Equipment:** _(smart trainer / power meter / HR monitor / gym access; for
  HYROX: SkiErg, rower, sled+turf, wall balls, sandbags and carry implements)_
- **Strength equipment:** _(gym / home weights — list what they own / none;
  ask once at the first strength touchpoint, then never re-ask)_
- **Power:** _(where watts are recorded — all rides / indoor only / none)_
- **Weight:** _(optional)_

## Health & safety

- **Safety screen:** _(Phase-1 cardiac screen — "clear, <date>", or what was
  flagged + the medical-check advice given. No hard prescriptions while a
  flag is open.)_
- **Injuries / history:** _(anything a coach must respect)_
- **Medications:** _(only what changes training — beta-blockers → prescribe by
  power/RPE not HR; anticoagulants → crash protocol changes; none = "none")_
- **Minor (16–17):** _(minimum coaching age is 16 — note which parent/guardian
  knows about the coaching; school schedule goes under Life context)_

## Communication

- **Language:** _(the language the athlete writes in — coach always replies in it)_
- **Style notes:** _(short/long answers, humor, how they take pushback)_
- **Plan ownership:** _(coach leads / shared decisions / athlete owns the plan
  and wants an advisor; independent of experience)_
- **Challenge preference:** _(supportive / balanced / direct disagreement)_
- **Evidence preference:** _(concise / explained / research cited for
  consequential prescriptions; leave neutral unless expressed)_
- **Health check-ins:** _(routine by default; "athlete-initiated" when they ask
  not to be asked about illness or niggles — they raise issues themselves)_

## Life context

_(Training fits around a life, not the other way round. The coach plans with
this, and updates it as life changes.)_

- **Work:** _(desk/shifts/travel; typical hours; stressful periods)_
- **Family:** _(partner, kids + ages; family time that training must respect)_
- **Fixed commitments:** _(weekly things that block training slots)_
- **Stress & sleep baseline:** _(normal bedtime, what wrecks sleep)_
- **Food:** _(eating pattern — vegetarian/vegan/allergies; what they eat on
  the bike today; for female athletes: menstrual function noted per reference §5)_
- **Other sports / activities:** _(gym, skiing; triathlon legs and HYROX running,
  stations and strength belong in the main plan, not under cross-training)_

## Connections

- **Activity recorder & route:** _(Garmin/Wahoo/other; direct activity download
  into intervals.icu verified with full duration/distance/power, or still
  Strava-only/date-only; whether planned-workout upload to the device is enabled)_
- **Recovery tracker:** _(none, or which one — Whoop/Garmin/Oura/Apple Watch/
  Polar…; ask. If none, coach recovery by asking + TSB, never by chasing a sync.
  HRV/sleep/readiness sync via intervals.icu wellness regardless of brand.)_
- **How they flag life/availability:** _(no calendar integration — athlete
  tells the coach about travel / busy weeks / family as it comes up)_

## Context

- **Recent training:** _(how consistent lately)_
- **Preferences & constraints:** _(e.g. no intensity on weekdays, hates VO2,
  prefers outdoor weekends)_

## Training background & response

- **Racing / endurance background:** _(years, level/category, typical seasons,
  meaningful past events — the athlete's story, not just files)_
- **Past coaching / plans:** _(what structure they have followed before)_
- **Typical sustainable volume:** _(normal week, biggest weeks/blocks, seasonal
  variation; reconcile their account with the observed history above)_
- **Long-session history:** _(what duration/frequency they tolerate, recovery
  afterward, and whether long sessions matter mentally)_
- **What has worked:** _(session types, block shapes, taper style, recovery rhythm)_
- **What has backfired:** _(load patterns, workouts, timing, injuries, burnout)_
- **Enjoys / avoids:** _(workouts they look forward to or dislike, and why)_
- **Recovery response:** _(how quickly they absorb intensity, volume, strength,
  heat, races; subjective signs that reliably predict readiness)_

## Strengths & weaknesses

_(The coach's assessment verdict, refreshed at block boundaries. Data side:
power-profile W/kg at 5s/1m/5m/20m vs benchmarks, phenotype, durability.
Athlete side: where they get dropped, what feels easy/hard. Current limiters —
the 1–2 being targeted this block — and the standing strengths to race to.
For HYROX, separate fresh running, stations/strength, compromised running,
standards/transitions and health/load constraints. Always dated, always with
the supporting numbers.)_

## Coach's read

_(The coach's own running notes on the athlete: what motivates them, how they
respond to load and to coaching, patterns worth remembering — e.g. "underreports
fatigue", "rides too hard on group rides", "responds well to clear daily
targets". Grows over time; prune what stops being true.)_
